Jade ("What am I looking at") - Jade 5.3.2 for Minecraft 1.18.2 by Snownee, which continues Waila/Hwyla by
ProfMobius and TehNut. https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jade

This client's Jade (net.minecraft.client.jade) is an adaptation of Jade's code to Minecraft 1.12: the overlay, its
themes and settings, and the vanilla providers (names, mod name, health and armor, crop growth, the harvest tool,
redstone, spawners, horses, item frames, enchanting power, player heads, note blocks, TNT, baby mobs). Its text
strings are Jade's English ones. Changes from the original: ported to the 1.12 code base, the settings screen is one
list of buttons, the harvest tool's tick sits on the tool icon.
